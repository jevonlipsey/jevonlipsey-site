Computational redistricting pipeline analyzing how legal Community of Interest (COI) mandates can be weaponized in district map generation. Built an automated dual-graph pipeline intersecting Census VTDs with citizen-submitted public testimony, calculating Total Community Preservation (TCP) across 20M+ MCMC ReCom steps to benchmark trade-offs against traditional compactness and Shannon entropy metrics.
